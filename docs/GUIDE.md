# Driving the demo — a 2-minute guide

[← back to the README](../README.md) · *No reading required to enjoy it — but here's how to get the most out of the first few minutes.*

The twin is **desktop only** (a banner appears below 1280 px) and runs in **dark mode**. Everything you see is driven by the simulation — there are no scripted animations.

## 1. Press **Lancer** (Start)

Nothing moves until you start the clock. One tick = one simulated day. Use the speed controls — **⏸ / ×1 / ×10 / Skip to end** — to fast-forward through seasons. At ×1 a full simulated year takes ~6 minutes, so **×10 is the comfortable cruising speed**.

## 2. Pick a strategy (preset)

Four starting points, top of the scenario panel:

- **Reference** — conventional Perche farming (the baseline).
- **Low-input** — reduced nitrogen & pesticides, cover crops, more hedgerows.
- **Intensive** — high inputs, hedgerow removal.
- **RCP4.5 drought** — a hotter, drier climate, no change in practice.

Switch between them and watch the **Hero KPIs** (top bar) and the scene react.

## 3. Pull the levers yourself

Below the presets, **6 management levers** (nitrogen, pesticides, tillage, cover crops, hedgerow management, grassland share) and **2 climate dials** apply **immediately**. Try nudging nitrogen up: input cost rises, and the biodiversity *Inputs* factor drops — every lever has a real downside, so the best setting is usually somewhere in the middle, not at an extreme.

A **starting-month** dropdown sets the season you begin in (it takes effect on the next launch).

## 4. Read the land

- **5 Hero KPIs** along the top — water reserve (% RU), soil carbon, biodiversity, crop yield, and margin — plus the *instrumentation contribution* (see the honest note in the [README](../README.md)).
- **3 thematic tabs** (Biodiversity / Climate & resources / Economy) — open one for the breakdown behind a KPI.
- **Yearly yield**: the crop yield only changes once per simulated year (at harvest), so run at ×10 to watch it swing between good years and droughts.

## 5. Inspect a sensor

Click any of the **5 instruments** in the scene (or in the sensor list) — weather station, eddy-covariance tower, piezometer, acoustic and camera-trap fauna sensors. A panel shows its **live measured reading and noise model**. The alerts threshold the *measured* value, not the model's hidden truth — that's the *sensor primacy* rule.

## 6. Arbitrate a recommendation

When a sensor detects something (a drought, a fauna anomaly, low profitability…), the decision engine proposes a move. **Win-win moves pop up**; **trade-offs wait in a passive list**. Each comes with a model-derived outcome projection — you **Validate**, **Ignore**, or defer it. The engine is honest: when profitability collapses it will even suggest *re-intensifying*, within a biodiversity guardrail.

## 7. Watch it breathe

Birds cross the scene and a heron settles in **only when the measured biodiversity index earns them**. If you run a healthy low-input strategy long enough, the sky fills up. Run the drought preset for a decade and it empties. No calendar, no scripting — a living readout of the model.
